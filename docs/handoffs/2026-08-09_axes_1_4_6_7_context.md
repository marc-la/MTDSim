# Axes 1, 4, 6 and 7 — context only: what each badge rests on, what has already been tried, and what a positive result would have to clear

## What this document is, and what it deliberately is not

**Context only.** The approach arrives in the session prompt; Marc brings the
ideas. This handoff therefore carries **no recommended approach and no validation
gate for work it has invented** — supplying either would pre-empt the thing it
exists to support.

What it does supply, per axis: the badge and what it actually rests on, what is
built, what has been swept and what each sweep returned, and — the load-bearing
part — **the bounds already established by measurement**, so that a proposal lands
against the real bar instead of re-deriving a wall someone already hit. Several of
these axes have absorbed thousands of runs. The fastest way to a positive result is
to know precisely which doors are shut and which are merely unopened.

**Read §1 before the per-axis sections.** The four axes share a badge and do not
share a reason for it, and the difference decides what kind of idea can move each.

## 1. The four are at DESIGNED for two different reasons

The criterion records this distinction and notes that the badge vocabulary cannot
encode it ([`../implementation/apt_model_criterion.md`](../implementation/apt_model_criterion.md)
§(b), "one distinction the badge vocabulary does not encode"):

- **Axis 1 records an *absence*** — a structure that runs, with no outcome ever
  shown. Nothing has been swept and found wanting; the outcome simply is not on
  record.
- **Axes 4, 6 and 7 record *measured negatives*** — a built, declared, swept,
  ablatable mechanism shown to operate **without conferring advantage**.

That difference is the whole strategic picture. On axis 1, a positive result may be
available from measurement alone. On axes 4, 6 and 7, the mechanism already exists
and has already been shown not to help, so a positive result requires changing
**what is measured**, **what the credit signal carries**, or **what the substrate
offers** — not adding another modulator to the stack. Five declared modulators have
been swept and every one narrows traversal (see the axis-3 handoff); a sixth of the
same shape is the move with the most evidence against it.

A second standing fact worth holding: a **measured negative is itself a
contribution**, and the criterion treats it as one. Several of these axes carry
transferable warnings that only a model with the capability could have produced.
"Positive result" need not mean "badge moves".

## 2. Axis 1 — Persistence (DESIGNED, and the one that is an absence)

**What the badge rests on.** A 15-tactic-place Petri campaign structure derived
from the Attack Flow corpus, with pre-intrusion structure composed in synthetically
(M6). One hundred coupled runs traversed it end-to-end. What is *not* on record is
persistence in **outcome** terms: 0/100 objective reaches, and effort does not
convert to breadth.

**What is built.** `distinct_place_curve` / `distinct_place_count`,
`deepest_successful_stage`, `deepest_visited_stage` (kept only as the saturated
comparator), `foothold_retentions`, `successes_per_distinct_host`,
`actions_per_distinct_host`, `first_success_stage`,
`advanced_after_first_success`, `blocked_fraction_trend`
([`measures.py`](../../src/mtdsim/l3_simulation/movement/measures.py) §1).

**The bounds already established — read these before proposing a measure.**

- **Kill-chain depth is withdrawn as saturated.** Every profile traverses to the
  objective stage of its own structure; `deepest_visited_stage` reads 3.0 ± 0.0 for
  all five profiles under both mappings, zero adjacent pairs separating.
- **Its replacement is *also* saturated under the go-forward mapping.**
  `deepest_successful_stage` returns the same value for **all 800** movement runs
  of experiment 2. The cause is structural, not power: seven tactics are dwell-only
  under `v2_partial`, including the objective band, so a stage-3 place can dispatch
  no verb and hold no success verdict — the measure's ceiling drops to 2
  ([`measurement_suite.md`](../implementation/pipeline/ogasp/measurement_suite.md)
  §(c)).
- **Foothold retention counts the wrong thing.** It counts footholds *severed*
  rather than retained, with the further artefact that the two application-layer
  mechanisms interrupt often and sever position **never**, so retention under them
  is total by the absence of any threat to it. Against the defences that do contest
  position, per-foothold retention at 200 s is **0.0–1.6 %**.
- **Records carry no host identity.** `foothold_retentions` therefore measures
  retention of *position*, not of a named host. `n_compromised` is a monotone count
  and does not close this — a count is not an identity.
- **Experiment 2 reported this axis moving to DEMONSTRATED and the move was
  withdrawn** on cross-examination, on the first two grounds above. The criterion
  records the near-miss deliberately, as the pre-registration discipline working.

**What a positive result would have to clear.** A progression measure that
discriminates inside the degenerate region and is not saturated by the mapping, or
an outcome that shows sustained staged advance rather than traversal. The coverage
curve (`distinct_place_curve`) is the one measure the suite says still
discriminates in the band the depth measure cannot see, and it currently leads by
default rather than by demonstration.

## 3. Axis 4 — Adaptivity to defender resistance (DESIGNED, measured negative)

**What is built.** The minimal adaptive loop: the substrate's verdict on each
dispatched action selects between success and failure transition-weight sets at the
current place (M2), and an MTD mutation severing the attacker's position throws the
net's state back (M1). Built at `48471b8`, verified in
[`runtime_verification.md`](../implementation/pipeline/ogasp/runtime_verification.md).

**What has been measured, and this is the sharpest negative on the instrument.**
Experiment 2 ran a **verdict-blind ablation arm** — an overlay whose value tables
are empty, so composition passes every destination through at 1.0 — on identical
seeds across the full matrix. **After 1 600 paired runs, no progression measure
separates it from the conditioned arm** on the pre-registered bar. At 2 000 s
breadth reaches one profile and three conditions; the profile half of the bar is
not met. Point estimates: conditioning helps nominally on four of five profiles
under no MTD and **hurts** on the fifth (`objective_none_c2` 4.10 against 5.40),
every interval overlapping.

The finding the pre-registration committed to reporting: **routing on the
substrate's verdict is approximately free**
([`experiment_02_findings.md`](../implementation/pipeline/ogasp/experiment_02_findings.md)
§11).

**One half of the axis's own next step is already discharged and did not move it.**
S1 named attacker-state-conditioned dynamic weights; the axis-7 learner supplies
them. The routing weights *do* update from experience, and the result was a sharper
version of the same verdict — friction falls, breadth falls with it.

**What is built and has never been run.** `interrupt_action_mix` appears nowhere
outside the suite record. `recovery_times`, `refoothold_times`, `refoothold_rate`
and `failure_routing_rate` exist; the refoothold pair was exercised in the
demonstration-arms workspace, the action-mix reader never anywhere. These are
exactly the three M8b measurements the axis names — pre/post-MTD action mix,
recovery time from a throw-back, and whether failure-conditioned routing redirects
effort — and they have not been reported.

**The standing boundary.** Adaptation here is outcome-reactive re-routing over
static weight sets. It does **not** condition on the defence itself; that is axis
8, which is closed on evidence.

## 4. Axis 6 — Incentive-driven rationality (DESIGNED, and closed by ruling)

**Marc closed this row on 2026-08-02.** The FINAL DISPOSITION in the criterion:
*"this row is DESIGNED, and that is where it ends for this project. Its attempted
implementations are recorded as negative results and full incentive rationality is
named as future work."* Any proposal here either accepts that closure or
deliberately reopens it, and reopening should be explicit rather than incidental.

**The two attempts and what each returned.**

- **Declared-duration utility modulator** (2026-07-29, 1 800 runs). Operates and
  changes behaviour; does not change MTD's measured effect. The anatomy is the
  finding: MTD's tax **is** strongly differentiated across tactics (18-fold spread
  in interrupt rate) but is levied in near-proportion to declared dwell (a roughly
  uniform ~9 % surcharge), and **a normalised utility ratio is invariant to a
  proportional inflation of its denominator**.
- **Iterated model** (2026-08-02, 4 200 runs). Repaired the R2 double-penalty
  defect measurably — 73–89 % of the blocked-fraction rise undone, successes per
  action roughly doubled — and still did not move the badge, because **the criterion
  that would have moved it was passed by a negative control**: the `declared` arm,
  which F6 proved by spike cannot see MTD at all (its factor table precomputes to
  30/30 bit-identical, and the MTD condition is not among its inputs). Its
  implementation has since been deleted.

**Why the row closed rather than waited — the substantive reason.** On this
substrate the attacker has **something to be rational about but nothing to be
rational toward**. Readiness is real, state-dependent and conditionable, but it is
*competence*, not *incentive*. Incentive requires a payoff to weigh: this attacker
reaches the objective zero times in 1 200 runs, and the verbs that cause compromise
produce nothing in the capability vocabulary, so nothing is ever banked.

**Three measured bounds on any future attempt.** Under the profiles' own nets,
`aggregate` places every tactic within one hop of an objective, and **14–38 % of
decision points sit at singleton out-sets** where the renormalised factor is exactly
1.000 whatever a mechanism computes. The capability channel resolves the whole
defence to a **two-bit register**. And the diversity family is **unreachable by
declaration** — what OS and Service Diversity destroy lives outside the guard the
capability vocabulary was transcribed from.

**The named future-work route collides with a feasibility finding.** The criterion
says a positive would need an attacker with an accumulable payoff — "a located
objective (the database set, or a genuinely targeted attacker)". The targeted
attacker was spiked and **the targeted network is not merely unexercised, it is not
constructible**: five blockers B1–B5, spanning substrate construction, a
commented-out termination, and a strategy with no live code path (IS-SCN-03)
([`targeted_attacker_feasibility.md`](../implementation/pipeline/ogasp/targeted_attacker_feasibility.md)
§4). B1–B5 are substrate changes, and S2 is the live constraint on them.

**What replaced the mechanism is a measurement**, explicitly not a badge move: the
attacker-disengagement reader — **Projected Campaign Effort**, **Abandonment
Effort** `A(k)`, the **Disengagement Frontier**. It scores an outcome (where the
attacker would have quit); the row asks whether the attacker *conditions on* cost.

## 5. Axis 7 — Learning capability (DESIGNED, and where the live positive is)

**What is built.** A within-run Laplace belief over the success/failure verdicts
observed at each tactic-place, entering routing as a declared exponent κ and
perishing by a declared fraction ρ on every MTD mutation. Declared, tiered, banded,
swept over 2 400 runs; the zero-capability arm is bit-identical to a run without the
mechanism.

**Three sweeps, in order, and what each settled.**

1. **The original learner** (2 400 runs). Operates — blocked fraction falls 91 % →
   21 % as capability rises, within runs, against an ablation that improves only
   slightly. Does not help — breadth falls 6.5 → 0.8 hosts. **Why is the finding**:
   the binary routing verdict is not a progress signal; scanning succeeds far more
   often than exploiting, so the belief correctly concludes reconnaissance pays and
   exploitation falls from 13 % of successes to 1 %.
2. **The readiness re-key** (4 600 runs). The learner re-keyed to `(destination
   tactic, precondition-satisfied?)`. **The representation was a genuine blocker and
   repairing it recovered exactly the ground the marginal key had lost** — breadth
   3.38 → 4.52 — **but the no-learning ablation sits at 4.60** and the readiness
   learner does not exceed it at any capability. Reward owns the ceiling;
   representation owned the collapse.
3. **Progress credit** (2026-08-02, **7 000 runs**, seven arms, 50 seeds). The
   credit-signal redesign the criterion names as the sole remaining item. **All five
   pre-registered conclusions not confirmed, two refuted in the opposite direction.**

**The progress-credit verdicts, because they bound the obvious next idea**
([`progress_credit_findings.md`](../implementation/pipeline/ogasp/progress_credit_findings.md)):

- **U1** badge gate — not confirmed. 4.54 ± 0.39 against ablation 4.11 ± 0.31,
  direction favourable at both MTD conditions, intervals overlapping.
- **U2** the credit repair like-for-like — not confirmed. Direction favours progress
  in **8 of 10** cells; neither pooled comparison separates. A power statement, not
  a mechanism statement.
- **U3 — the uncomfortable one, and the axis's live problem.** The mechanism
  separates cleanly from `control_asymptotic` (JSD 0.2804) and **does not separate
  from `control_matched`** (0.1196) — a declared static bias matched to the
  learner's own observed aggression. *"On the criterion an examiner asks first — is
  this learning, or a lookup with extra steps — the answer here is not yet
  established."*
- **U4** — sign opposite to prediction; MTD's absolute effect marginally larger
  against the learner.
- **U5 — refuted with separation.** ρ = 0 (never forget) is **worse** than ρ = 0.5:
  1.06 ± 0.18 against 1.49 ± 0.19, CI-disjoint. Forgetting *helps*, because a
  learner that never forgets becomes confidently committed to a policy the defence
  has already invalidated. This killed the C2 type-discipline recommendation as
  stated. **Do not adopt ρ = 0.**

**The one separated positive result in 7 000 runs, and it is a lead, not a
finding.** On `v1_ckc_total` with no MTD, `progress_r50` reaches **1.70 ± 0.29**
against ablation **0.44 ± 0.08** and acceptance **0.47 ± 0.07** — CI-disjoint
against both, a ~3.6× improvement. `v1_ckc_total` is the mapping that runs at
60–98 % blocked, i.e. the one where the CTI-derived order and the substrate's
procedural order disagree most.

The coherent reading — **explicitly a hypothesis, not a finding** — is that
progress credit pays in proportion to how badly the two orders mismatch. That is
directly a claim about porting behaviourally-grounded attackers onto procedurally
rigid substrates, which is the thesis's own subject. **It carries no gate by
pre-registration and must not be reported as a badge argument**; it needs its own
pre-registration with `v1_ckc_total` named as the decision cell in advance. The
findings record lists it as successor priority 1.

**One profile is harmed, and the cause was identified before the sweep ran.**
Progress credit helps four profiles and hurts `objective_none_c2` badly (3.40 →
2.02): the pivot (`lateral-movement` / `ENUM_HOST`) held a belief of 0.138, and
suppressing the pivot closes the campaign early. Recorded as a watch item in
advance, which is why it is diagnosable. Successor priority 2 is to fix the pivot,
then re-test U2.

**A measurement warning that binds any evaluation here.** On every friction-shaped
measure the two representations are indistinguishable to three decimal places,
differing only on breadth. The within-run blocked-fraction measure **cannot
discriminate between representations** and must never be read as evidence for one.

**And a documentation gap to be aware of when reading the criterion.** The
criterion's axis 7 has never been amended for the progress-credit sweep — it still
states that a progress-carrying credit signal is "the sole remaining item".
`progress_credit` appears nowhere in it. Read the findings record, not the
criterion, for this axis's current state.

## 6. Constraints that bind any proposal, whatever its shape

- **The degenerate region.** At the 200 s operating interval neither attacker
  completes the objective, so success-rate-shaped measures are pinned at zero and
  discriminate nothing. Evidence must be breadth- or time-shaped, or come from
  outside the region and say so.
- **Ten seeds separate almost nothing.** Every aggregate goes through
  `interval_report`; `ordering_supported` is the gate, never the sorted means.
- **Reader versus mechanism.** A reader changes nothing and moves no badge — axis 5
  declined the move three times on exactly this ground. A mechanism raises the S2
  freeze question and a comparability argument.
- **Pre-registration and the stopping rule.** Conclusions, criteria and directions
  committed before any output, in their own commit; nothing re-specified after it
  fails to discriminate. Two of this project's most useful results exist because a
  criterion was allowed to fail.
- **Run the second control.** U3 is the standing demonstration: a mechanism that
  separates from a weak control and not from a matched one has not been shown to do
  anything. One control invites the near-miss.
- **Scores move on evidence only** — never change the model, weights, mapping or
  metrics to improve a row (S6;
  [`../workflows/guardrails.md`](../workflows/guardrails.md)).
- **Envelope, not actor**; within-substrate comparability only; cross-arm
  comparisons effort-denominated, never time-denominated.
- Determinism (SIM-05); Australian English; branch per session; commit locally;
  **never push**.

## 7. Where the unopened doors are, stated plainly

Not recommendations — an inventory, so a proposal can be placed against it.

- **Axis 7's Lead 1** is the only separated positive result the axis has produced
  in ~14 000 runs, and it sits outside the gated cell with a named successor action.
- **Axis 4's three M8b readers are built and unreported.** They are the axis's own
  named measurements and nothing has run them.
- **Axis 1's coverage curve leads by default**, not by demonstration; the two depth
  measures are saturated and retention measures the wrong object.
- **Axis 6 is closed by ruling**, and its named future-work route is blocked by a
  substrate that will not construct.
