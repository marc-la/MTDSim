**Current model and results, used as the yardstick (from the ch4/ch5 prose):**
- **Model:** 38 attack flows feed an attack graph over 15 tactics, split into four attack profiles c1–c4 by two questions (stole data? impeded systems?). Each profile is a GSPN: base weights are flow shares, each APT group counts once (29 of 38 flows), one token, and a pre-intrusion overlay in c3 and c4. A failure matrix reweights after a failed attack action. The tactic-to-action mapping has 7 unmapped tactics. Tactic durations: 6 from MTDSim, 9 by judgement, 45 s the sensitive one. The vulnerability memory triples the odds per earlier success, lasts the whole run, and learns vulnerabilities, not MTD.
- **Table 6.1:** properties 1–4 ticked (1 kept from the baseline), 6 and 7 partial (6 kept from the baseline), 5 and 8 crossed.
- **Results with no MTD (5.2):** the profiles divide their steps differently, with 26–47 % unmapped. The baseline attacker has one opening over four steps; the profiles have 179–947 distinct openings over eight steps. The baseline attacker's ASP is 5–16× and its NCR 2.4–3.3× the profiles'. MTTC: 9 100 s for the baseline against 9 700–11 300 s, so "just shorter". Attack rate is 1.709 against 0.680–1.228, and attack confidentiality 82.2 % against 89.4–98.1 %.
- **Disruption (5.3.1):** attack actions blocked per deployment is 1 against 0.35–0.39 on the host layer and 0.95 against 0.15 on the service layer. The APT model loses 242–324 s per host-layer deployment and the baseline 0. The baseline loses 493 s per service-diversity deployment and the APT model 60 s. The baseline's time lost is below zero elsewhere: the first deployment brings its first compromise 204–232 s forward.
- **Ranking (5.3.2):** no MTD ranks first against both attackers. ρ = 0.01 [−0.19, 0.05], and −0.24 to 0.25 at every interval. The host layer beats the service layer against the APT model at every interval; the reverse holds for the baseline. User shuffle is below zero against the APT model. Random and alternative favour the APT model, MTDShield the baseline; MTDShield picks service diversity in ≥70 % of decisions.
- **Profiles (5.3.3):** at 200 s the profiles lie within 0.14 of one another, and the baseline lies ≥0.24 from the nearest profile. c3 is furthest from the baseline at 500 and 1 000 s.
- **Ablations (5.4):** without the attack profiles NCR goes from 0.171 to 0.189 (d −0.28); 29 of 60 configurations are not negligible, 7 negligible and 24 inconclusive. Against ten random partitions the profiles compromise more, d 0.25–1.47, after the 2026-10-07 redraw. The failure matrix changes where the attacker goes after a failure (0.76–0.83 against 0.08–0.10 back to reconnaissance), and its effect on NCR is negligible. The memory raises exploit success (0.73 against 0.69; 0.92 against 0.69 at one service per OS), and its effect on NCR is negligible.
