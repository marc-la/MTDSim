# The APT criterion — an instrument commissioned, worked, and honestly closed

**Span:** 2026-07-27 → 2026-08-13. **Prompts:** #38, #41, #45, #48, #55, #56,
#77, #78, #80, #90, #104. The instrument itself is
[`../../apt_model_criterion.md`](../../apt_model_criterion.md) (loaded every
session); this thread records its life as a decision object.

**Genesis (#38, 2026-07-27), with its constraints stated at birth.** Point 6 of
the six-point metabolisation of the 27-Jul supervisor meeting: derive a
structured criterion from the APT literature Marc had already reviewed —
Alshamrani 2019, Cho 2020's axes, Jalowski 2026 "points at the same issue that I
am looking at" — wire it into every session's context, and keep it honest:
"well structured, well thought out, contained, and **isn't promising the
world**. My work yes will not address all the points … but it captures the
missing essence of what is missing." The fixed-before-scoring discipline and
the not-addressed rows being as visible as the positives were both in the
commissioning prompt, not added by the implementation.

**Marc's own vocabulary for the axes (#55, 2026-08-01; #77, 2026-08-09).** The
plain-language definitions he uses to his supervisor differ usefully from the
instrument's formal rows — e.g. axis 4 as "**strategic robustness**": "even if
the attack suffers a failure signal, there are a plurality of strategies the
attack model prefers to bounce back to … this is the framing I see myself
writing in my dissertation" (#80, #90). The criterion's later dispositions
(2026-08-11/13) post-date and supersede these prompts *as record*, but the
robustness phrasing remains the write-up voice for the methodology chapter —
a drafting resource, not a drift flag (see dispositions § flags).

**The pushes and the kills.** #41/#45 are the demonstration pushes (how does
each DESIGNED row get to DEMONSTRATED), and carry two designs that were never
built: a stealth *state* modulating weights, and MTD-awareness as a [0,1]
knowledge dial — with the standing bound "not looking to go into deep RL".
#48 aims experiment 2 at the axes. #56 consolidates the direction (stealth as
tempo through the network metrics; the disengagement/downtime balance). #77 is
the kill record in Marc's own words: axis 1 accepted as characteristic-not-metric,
axis 8 killed on calibration circularity (see
[`axis8_rise_and_fall.md`](axis8_rise_and_fall.md)), and #78 the
persistence-has-no-contrast-class argument later ratified into
[`../../../notes/ch7_discussion/persistence_duration_premise.md`](../../../notes/ch7_discussion/persistence_duration_premise.md).
#104 (2026-08-13) is the last movement: the pivot-kernel frame for axis 4,
answered by the structural probe and the declined kernel the criterion's
2026-08-13 amendment records.

**The one reading rule this thread adds:** the criterion was never the goal.
"The APT criterion is a tool to produce attack model fidelity, but it should not
be viewed as the end in itself" (#53) — the badge economy exists to keep claims
honest, not to be maximised.
