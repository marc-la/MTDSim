\section*{Use of AI Tools}
\label{sec:ai_use}

Generative AI was used in the preparation of this literature review. The sole tool was Claude Opus 4.7 Adaptive (Anthropic), used throughout as a drafting, formatting, and editorial assistant operating under the author's direction.
Its uses were: assisting with the drafting and restructuring of prose across the review's sections; retrieving and formatting bibliographic entries and producing IEEE-compliant LaTeX and BibTeX; and proofreading for grammar, internal consistency, and referencing-style conformance.

Because generative tools are prone to producing plausible but incorrect output, all AI-assisted material was subjected to a structured two-stage verification audit by the author. The first stage confirmed the existence and bibliographic metadata of every reference against primary publisher records (IEEE Xplore, the ACM Digital Library, Elsevier ScienceDirect, Springer, MD      PI, USENIX, and arXiv). The second verified every quotation, statistic, page locator, and characterisation of a source against the original source documents. Errors characteristic of generative output — most commonly confabulated author given names and fabricated page ranges — were identified and corrected during this audit, and any claim that could not be substantiated against a primary source was cut or hedged rather than retained.

All research direction, problem formulation, the selection and discrimination of sources, and the analytical synthesis and interpretation presented in this review are the author's own, developed in consultation with the supervisor. The author reviewed, edited, and accepted or rejected every AI-generated suggestion, and takes full responsibility for the entire content of this review, including all claims, citations, and quotations. Any remaining errors are the author's own.
