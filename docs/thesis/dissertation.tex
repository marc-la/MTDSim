% PREAMBLE prose to draft: state the research question (what greater attacker

The evaluation answers a single question --- what greater attacker fidelity
implies for current methods of evaluating moving target defence --- on two
dimensions, and scores every comparison it reports on one three-grade instrument
(Table~\ref{tab:eval-factors} fixes the factors both dimensions draw from).
% The instrument (magnitude / ordering / recommendation) is specified in full in
% the grading note; restate its three grades here claim-first before the table.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Dimensions of the evaluation --- the factors both experiment families
  draw their cells from. Seeds are a claim-class convention stated once (ten
  supports a rank comparison, not a significance test), not a factor.}
  \label{tab:eval-factors}
  \begin{tabular}{p{0.20\textwidth} p{0.42\textwidth} p{0.30\textwidth}}
    \hline
    \textbf{Factor} & \textbf{Levels} & \textbf{Role} \\
    \hline
    Attacker arm & inherited scripted; profiled campaign envelope
      ($\times$ objective profiles) & the manipulation under test \\
    Defence & none; each mechanism singly; shuffle-only pair; diversity-only
      pair; shuffle $+$ diversity; execution schemes & the ranked object; the
      lineage headlines are stated over exactly these \\
    Network scale & small / medium / large & the axis each fidelity extension is
      designed to show or vanish on \\
    Connection density & held vs.\ varied at fixed node count & settles the
      lineage's unresolved size-vs-density confound \\
    Mutation tempo & operating interval; relaxed interval & the degenerate region
      lives here; grades are stated with the tempo they were taken at \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
