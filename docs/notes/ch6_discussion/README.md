# ch6_discussion — notes feeding the Discussion chapter

## What this chapter does

The discussion chapter answers **"so what?"** — it interprets the results against the research question and the field, not merely restates them. Its two movements: *impact* (what changes because this exists — does the post-ingress reversal hold, and what does that say about where MTD research should aim; how the findings read back onto the coverage-bias motivation the introduction opened with) and *limitations owned in one place* (corpus thinness, operator concentration, declared parameters, the detection-regime boundary — consolidated and weighed, not scattered as per-chapter disclaimers). It is also where the honest scope of every claim is settled: what the evidence licenses, what it merely suggests, and what remains conjecture. The conclusion chapter then compresses this into impact-plus-next-step. (Whole-document guidance: [`../_writing_guide.md`](../_writing_guide.md).)

Current notes: [`learning_without_context.md`](learning_without_context.md) — an attacker that learns from outcomes alone routes around a procedural constraint rather than satisfying it. [`procedural_mismatch_artefact.md`](procedural_mismatch_artefact.md) — a host simulator's encoded procedure manufactures attacker failure that evaluations misread as attacker weakness. [`instruments_fail_silently.md`](instruments_fail_silently.md) — four progression/credit measures failed silently when the attacker's shape changed; re-validate instruments on attacker change. [`state_bounds_measurable_disruption.md`](state_bounds_measurable_disruption.md) — a defence can only destroy the kind of state the attacker model carries, so the attacker's state representation bounds what the evaluation can measure. [`refusing_the_baseline_race.md`](refusing_the_baseline_race.md) — the profiled attacker is a diagnostic census of what the field's scripted attacker cannot express, not a competitor to out-score; its weaker headline performance is the condition of the study, not its verdict.

What lands here: *interpretation and limitations synthesis* — what the results mean against the research question, how the post-ingress reversal (or its absence) reads back onto the field's coverage bias, the consolidated limitations argument (corpus thinness, operator concentration, declared parameters, detection-regime mismatch), and honest-scope statements that span chapters.
