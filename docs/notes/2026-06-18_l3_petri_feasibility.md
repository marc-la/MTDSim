# Encoding the four GASP classes as Petri nets — a feasibility, literature and design study

> **Provenance banner.** A feasibility + analysis + design study, not a build
> record and not chapter prose. It answers a single question — *can the four
> L2 GASP classes be encoded as Petri nets whose analytical evaluation
> (CTMC → MTTC / ASR / reward in closed form) is the primary payoff?* — and
> renders a verdict with conditions. The substrate it studies is the canonical
> L2 GASP ([`../specs/02_gasp_schema.md`](../specs/02_gasp_schema.md),
> [`../../data/gasp/`](../../data/gasp/)); the prior art it supersedes is the
> 2026-05-02 SNAKES primer (on `feat/replay-viz`, built against the stale GAP
> v0.4). The four candidate encodings (§5) were generated by a multi-agent
> design pass; the judgement, the five-lens critique (§6) and the verdict (§8)
> are this study's synthesis, cross-checked against
> [`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) and
> [`../specs/architecture.md`](../specs/architecture.md). The closest external
> precedent, **Mendonça/Cho/Kim 2023**, is now extracted
> ([`../extractions/mendonca2023.md`](../extractions/mendonca2023.md)) and folded
> into §3/§6; **Cai 2016 is off-limits** (cannot be obtained) and is cited only
> second-hand via Mendonça and the primer. The implementation plan this study justifies is
> the handoff at
> [`../handoffs/2026-06-18_l3_ogasp_petri_implementation.md`](../handoffs/2026-06-18_l3_ogasp_petri_implementation.md).

## Why this is worth recording

Jin steered the project toward a SNAKES Petri-net formalisation on 24 April
2026, and the architecture has carried it ever since as a *candidate alternative
analytical substrate, parallel-not-primary* at L4
([`../specs/architecture.md`](../specs/architecture.md) §(f)). This study is the
moment that candidate is taken seriously enough to ask whether it actually works
on the artefact L2 produced — and the honest answer is **conditional**, in a way
that is itself the contribution. The feasibility verdict is **GO-CONDITIONAL**:
analytical Petri-net evaluation of the four GASP classes is feasible, but only at
a granularity that discards much of the technique-level and AND/OR structure the
thesis foregrounds, and only with firing rates that the corpus does not licence
as anything but notional. The three conditions that make it defensible — a
declared notional-rate regime reported as sensitivity bands, a fidelity
cross-validator that *measures* what the abstraction costs, and an explicit
architecture-positioning change — are the load-bearing methodological moves a
viva will probe. Recording them now, before any code is written, is what keeps
the workstream from quietly over-claiming a "second route to the same MTTC" that
[`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) already says is
not available.

## The substance

### 1. The question, sharpened by two fixed decisions

Two decisions are fixed (Marc, this session) and they set the entire shape of
the study:

1. **All four canonical classes** — `pure_steal`, `pure_impediment`,
   `double_extortion`, `infrastructure_setup` — not three. The fourth,
   `infrastructure_setup`, has **zero impact and zero exfiltration techniques**
   (it is a defender-eviction signature, [§4](#4-what-l2-actually-hands-forward)
   / [`./2026-05-29_l2_synthesis.md`](./2026-05-29_l2_synthesis.md) F3), so it
   has *no natural "objective reached" absorbing marking* and must be modelled
   with a **non-objective absorbing condition** (eviction / C2-established /
   max-dwell).
2. **Analytical evaluation is the primary payoff.** The point is a continuous-
   time Markov chain (CTMC) giving steady-state, mean-time-to-compromise (MTTC)
   and attack-success-rate (ASR) in **closed form**, not by Monte-Carlo. This
   makes **state-space size / tractability the binding constraint** — the design
   must *earn* tractability through abstraction, not assume it.

Everything below is conditioned on those two choices. A simulation-primary
framing (the primer's other half) would make most of the tractability anxiety
evaporate; the analytical-primary mandate is what makes this a hard problem.

### 2. Why a Petri net at all?

The case rests on one structural fact: **a stochastic Petri net (SPN) with
exponential firing has a reachability graph that is isomorphic to a CTMC**
(Murata 1989). That isomorphism is the whole payoff, because it converts every
metric the simulator estimates by sampling into a closed-form linear-algebra
problem on the generator matrix `Q`:

- **MTTC** = expected time to absorption = `τ = −Q_T⁻¹ · 1` over the transient
  (non-absorbing) markings, with the absorbing set chosen as the breach marking.
- **ASR(T)** = `Pr[breach by T]` = `(e^{QT})_{M0, S_abs}` by matrix exponential
  or transient ODE integration.
- **Steady-state / availability** = `π` solving `πQ = 0` (when ergodic).
- **Expected reward** (RoA, attack-path-exposure) attaches as a Stochastic
  Reward Net (SRN) reward function: `r̄ = Σ rᵢ πᵢ`, and finite-horizon accrued
  reward `E[∫₀ᵀ r(M(s)) ds]`.

Against a procedurally-scripted or sampling-based attacker, *every* evaluation
question is a Monte-Carlo experiment with statistical-power problems; against an
SPN/SRN attacker, the same questions are answered exactly, "with statistical
noise replaced by structural truncation error" (the primer's framing). That is
the argument for Petri nets over the current L3 sampling traversal: not
expressiveness, but **analytical tractability of the metrics that matter**.

This is not a speculative move — it is a well-trodden methodology. There is an
established lineage of using exactly SPN / GSPN / SRN to evaluate *and compare*
MTD mechanisms analytically (§3). What is novel here is the **direction**: that
literature models the *defender / MTD system* with an abstract attacker; this
thesis inverts it, putting a **CTI-grounded attacker** (the GASP) in the net and
treating MTD as an exogenous competing transition. The inversion is the
contribution and, as §6 shows, the source of the hardest problem (rate
grounding).

**What a Petri net does *not* buy**, stated up front so the rest reads honestly:
it does not produce a more "true" attacker (the validation claim stays
*"behavioural fidelity changes the answer"*, not *"the model is true"* —
[`../specs/architecture.md`](../specs/architecture.md) §(j)); it does not give
numbers comparable to the DES substrate's MTTC (§6.4); and at realistic class
sizes it does not stay analytically tractable *without* an abstraction that
costs fidelity (§6.1).

the disposition for this thesis.

Detection numbers are EDR ground truth, not MTD. |

Three lessons fall straight out of the table and drive the design:

- **L1 — Tactic granularity is the field's standard tractability lever** (Cai,
  Cho, Rodríguez, Outkin all keep the state space small by working at
  tactic/phase or per-step granularity, never per-technique). The thesis's
  124-node-DAG attacker is *larger and more structured* than anything in this
  literature, so the tractability problem is genuinely harder here.
- **L2 — Nobody grounds attacker firing rates from frequency data.** Where rates
  are grounded at all (Outkin), they come from *detection-probability*
  engagement data on the *defender* side. Bland concedes notional rates as an
  open problem. This thesis has *no* corpus-grounded attacker rate (§6.2) —
  which is the literature's unsolved problem inherited, not a local defect.
- **L3 — The CTMC payoff is real and precedented, but it is a *second
  substrate*.** Every analytical work here computes its metrics on its own
  Markov model, never compares the number to a DES simulation of the same
  system. That boundary is exactly
  [`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) §(d), and it
  binds here (§6.4).

### 4. What L2 actually hands forward

The encoding is constrained by five facts about the GASP substrate, all
load-bearing:

- **Four surface subgraphs**, sized 98 / 62 / 57 / 39 nodes and 413 / 254 /
  225 / 148 edges (`pure_steal` / `pure_impediment` / `double_extortion` /
  `infrastructure_setup`; [`../specs/02_gasp_schema.md`](../specs/02_gasp_schema.md)
  §(d)). **Every one exceeds the primer's ~10–20-dynamic-node analytical
  tractability bound.**
- **They are weighted overlays over a shared substrate, not four disjoint
  graphs** ([`./2026-05-29_l2_synthesis.md`](./2026-05-29_l2_synthesis.md) F1):
  a 16-technique backbone sits in all four, the node sets union to 100 % of the
  GAP, pairwise node Jaccard is 0.295–0.422. The differentiation lives in
  *tactic-share weighting and per-class long tails*, not in *which techniques
  exist*. This is the single most important fact: **"four profiles → four nets"
  is really "one shared structure, four parameterisations."**
- **The recurring core is thin** (F2): at `obs ≥ 2` each class collapses to
  21–34 nodes, and `double_extortion`'s entire impact column disappears (every
  ransomware family uses its own encryption variant; no impact technique recurs
  across ≥ 2 flows). 88 % of GAP edges are single-observation;
  `observation_count` maxes at 4, mean 1.15.
- **`infrastructure_setup` has no objective** (F3): zero impact, zero
  exfiltration. Its absorbing condition is a defender-eviction artefact, not an
  attacker goal — so its MTTC is a *different metric* ("time to foothold or
  eviction") from the other three classes', and cannot be pooled into one
  comparison table without a caveat.
- **The lossless layer carries AND/OR; the surface subgraph mostly does not.**
  [`../specs/01_gap_schema.md`](../specs/01_gap_schema.md) Decision 2 names the
  *intended* Petri encoding as "actions → transitions, conditions → places,
  operators → join/split", preserving AND/OR — but that logic lives in the
  per-flow YAMLs ([`../../data/gap/flows/`](../../data/gap/flows/)), and in v0.5
  it is largely **vestigial**: all edges are `type: effect`, all conditions are
  pass-through, only 14 OR-operators corpus-wide. The non-trivial logic that
  survives is **AND-synchronisation** (e.g. the Tesla golden's three-input
  AND-join into T1496).

And one prohibition that governs the rates, already written into the substrate
spec: **`observation_count` is a recurrence count, explicitly *not* a transition
probability** — "weights are unnormalised counts… a node's out-edges sum to
nothing, and the graph is not a Markov chain… seeding L3 transition
probabilities directly from edge weights — **INVALID as-is**"
([`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) §(f)). Any
Petri-net rate grounding has to clear this bar (§6.2).

### 5. Four candidate encodings

A design pass generated four encoding families. Each is the strongest form of
its idea; the point is the *binding constraint* each one hits.

| | **D1 — tactic-place SPN** | **D2 — faithful workflow-net** | **D3 — per-technique single-token CTMC on a curated slice** | **D4 — one shared SRN, four rate/reward vectors** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Place = | ATT&CK tactic (≤15) | control-flow state (arc/condition place) | a technique (one-hot token) | ATT&CK tactic (≤15), "achieved-set" 1-safe |
| Transition = | a GAP edge (tactic→tactic) | an attacker **action** (timed) + AND/OR immediates | a GAP edge in the slice | an inter-tactic edge; AND/OR immediates |
| Multi-class | one net, class-coloured tokens | four nets, block-diagonal per flow | four small separate nets | **one net, four rate/reward vectors** (the F1 design) |
| State space | ≤15 (uncoloured) / 39–98 (coloured) | ~100/flow reduced; **~10¹⁸ if concurrency honoured** | ~10/class | **~10³–10⁴ shared**, solved once, re-used 4× |
| Analytically solvable? | yes (trivially) | **only** under per-flow + single-token + 1-safe | yes (trivially) | yes (conditional on 1-safe + tactic-quotient) |
| Binding constraint | **fidelity collapse** (uncoloured loses the technique distinction; colouring loses the size win; AND/OR unrepresentable) | **faithfulness vs tractability are mutually exclusive** for the two big classes | **slice-selection defensibility — fails on 2 of 4 classes** (`double_extortion`, `infrastructure_setup` have no recurring objective path) | **discrimination-as-input** (class differences are the weights you put in) + tactic-quotient erases F2 long tails |
| Implements GAP Decision 2 (AND/OR)? | no | **yes, exactly** | no | mostly no |

The diagnosis writes itself once the two fixed decisions (all four classes;
analytical-primary) are applied:

- **D2 is eliminated as the *primary analytical* substrate.** It is the *most*
  faithful (it literally implements Decision 2 and honours the Tesla AND-golden)
  but it is analytically solvable only after a single-token reduction that
  throws away the concurrency it exists to preserve; honouring AND-fork
  concurrency takes `pure_steal` to ~10¹⁸ markings. It is a **simulation-fidelity
  substrate**, not an analytical one.
- **D3 is eliminated as a *four-class* solution.** It is trivially solvable, but
  by its own analysis its mechanical slice rule fails on two of the four
  classes: `double_extortion`'s objective sits on `observation_count = 1` edges
  (the `obs ≥ 2` core *excludes* the objective), and `infrastructure_setup` has
  no objective at all. It is a clean tool for `pure_steal` and `pure_impediment`
  only.
- **D1 and D4 both clear the analytical-tractability bar for all four classes**,
  both by quotienting techniques onto ≤15 tactic-places. **D4 dominates D1**: it
  is the design the F1 finding directly demands (one shared structure, four
  parameterisations — the synthesis note's own verdict), it computes the
  reachability set once and re-uses it across four cheap re-solves, and it is an
  SRN (so RoA / path-exposure attach as rewards — the Cai/Cho/Mendonça line).
  D1's own verdict concedes it is best positioned as "the abstracted baseline the
  real encoding beats."

### 6. The hard critique — five lenses

The recommended design (D4) is not adopted before being attacked. Five lenses,
each cross-checked against the specs.

#### 6.1 Tractability — does the analytical payoff actually survive at class scale?

**Verdict: survives, but only because the abstraction is aggressive — and the
abstraction is the cost, not the compute.** D4 holds the reachability set at
~10³–10⁴ tangible states via four stacked reductions (tactic-quotient → ≤15
places; 1-safe achieved-set marking → order-ideals not 2¹⁵; one shared
reachability graph; GSPN vanishing-marking collapse). A dense `−Q_T⁻¹·1` at 10⁴
is a sparse linear solve in seconds. The *per-technique* alternative floors at
2³⁹ ≈ 5.5 × 10¹¹ (`infrastructure_setup`) and is categorically dead. So the
state space is not the problem; **what the tactic-quotient throws away** is. This
is exactly the field's known story: state-space explosion is *the* binding
constraint on Petri-net security models (Bland's own related work; the SRN
dependability literature), and the standard mitigations are hierarchical /
fixed-point / coarse-granularity — D4's "solve the shared reachability once,
re-use four times" is a flavour of the hierarchical move. **GO** on tractability,
with the cost booked under §6.5.

#### 6.2 Rate grounding & provenance — the deepest issue (and the most constructive)

**Verdict: MAJOR. The edge weights cannot ground the firing rates — for two
distinct reasons, and the deeper one is empirical.** This is the question that
matters most, so it is worth separating the two arguments that are usually run
together.

**Reason A — formal (the spec already forbids it).**
[`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) §(f) is explicit:
`observation_count` is a *recurrence* count — "how often analysts drew this
dependency across reported incidents" — and seeding transition rates/probabilities
from it is *"INVALID as-is"* because the weights are unnormalised, a node's
out-edges sum to nothing, and "the graph is not a Markov chain." It is biased
toward well-documented, successful campaigns; it is not efficacy, not a
transition probability, not causal strength. *Even if it were dense*, normalising
it into firing rates would read adversary-optimality and likelihood into what is
only "how often it was drawn."

**Reason B — empirical (the sparsity argument, and it is the stronger one).**
Set the formal prohibition aside and ask only for an *ordinal* signal — "which
dependencies are more well-trodden?" The corpus cannot even give that. The
verified `observation_count` distribution over all 478 GAP v0.5 edges:

| `observation_count` | edges | share |
|---|--:|--:|
| 1 | 419 | **87.7 %** |
| 2 | 52 | 10.9 % |
| 3 | 3 | 0.6 % |
| 4 | 4 | 0.8 % |

max = 4, mean = 1.15; per class the shape is identical (`obs ≥ 2` is only
14–24 % of each class's edges). The distribution is a **near-degenerate spike at
1**: ~88 % of edges are tied at the bottom, and the entire "signal" is 52 edges
at 2 and seven edges at 3–4. So the *ordinal-emphasis* residue floated in the
first draft of this study ("more-observed dependencies fire faster") is, on the
data, almost vacuous — it can separate at most the 12 % of edges with `obs ≥ 2`
from the rest, and barely orders within that. **The honest conclusion is stronger
than the spec's prohibition: the rates are *purely notional* — the corpus does
not even yield a usable ordinal.** This is not a build defect: the GAP is
Attack-Flow-only over 38 hand-curated incidents, and a technique→technique
dependency recurs only when *independent* incident reports happen to draw the
*same* pair — which is rare, because incidents differ in their specific
techniques. It is the L1 thinness finding (88 % single-observation) propagating
straight into L2; corpus growth is the only lever, and it is slow.

**The constructive consequence — this is the useful half.** Since the *rates*
cannot be grounded, do not claim the analytical MTTC is a *timing* number. The
corpus does ground the **structure** — the nodes, the edges (each a real
analyst-drawn dependency), the AND/OR operators, and crucially *which edges each
class contains* (the per-class surface subgraph). So:

- **Set rates uniform/notional and read the analytical MTTC as a *structural*
  metric** — expected number of steps/hops to absorption over the corpus-grounded
  topology, ASR as reachability-by-horizon. The four-class discrimination then
  comes from *graph structure* (path length, branching, per-class edge
  enablement) — which **is** data — not from a hand-set rate vector — which is
  not. This is *more* defensible, and it **partly defuses §6.5's
  discrimination-as-input critique**: under uniform rates the grounded input is
  the per-class edge-set, and the un-grounded rate vector — the thing §6.5 worries
  you are "putting in by hand" — is removed from the discrimination entirely.
- This sits cleanly inside the comparability framing the spec *already* blesses:
  [`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) §(f) calls
  "MTD-efficacy as how much MTD perturbs *typical observed attack workflow*"
  **valid (with the framing stated)**. A structural steps-to-absorption MTTC,
  perturbed by an MTD reset, is exactly that reading.
- **If a genuine *timing* MTTC (seconds) is wanted, the rates must be exogenous**
  — Outkin-style engagement/dwell data — declared and sensitivity-swept, with the
  EDR-vs-MTD calibration caveat ([`../specs/architecture.md`](../specs/architecture.md)
  §(j)). The corpus grounds *structure*; *timing* has to be imported, never
  manufactured from `observation_count`.

This is precisely the Mendonça 2023 parameterisation stance
([`../extractions/mendonca2023.md`](../extractions/mendonca2023.md) Concept 3):
ground what the data gives, *declare-and-sweep* what it does not — with the
asymmetry that Mendonça's groundable parameters are *defender* rates (a
literature exists), whereas the thesis's un-groundable ones are *attacker* rates,
for which no frequency-grounded source exists at all. **Go-condition #1 therefore
strengthens:** the baseline is **uniform/notional rates with a structural-MTTC
reading**, `observation_count` admitted at most as a *cosmetic three-tier
tie-break* (`1 / 2 / ≥3`), every number a sensitivity band, all written into
[`../specs/provenance.md`](../specs/provenance.md) **before** any number is
quoted.

#### 6.3 Markov semantics — is a CTMC even the right object?

**Verdict: MAJOR, and partly philosophical.** The CTMC payoff *requires*
exponential (memoryless) firing. Real attacker dwell is not exponential, and the
GAP is explicitly *not* a Markov chain. So the CTMC is a **modelling overlay** —
its Markov property is *asserted by the SRN formalism*, not *discovered in the
corpus*. That is legitimate (every SPN/SRN paper does the same) but it must be
*stated*, or a reader infers the GAP was treated as an estimated stochastic
process, which §(f) forbids. The sharper consequence: the CTMC MTTC (expected
time over a traversal to an absorbing marking) is **not the same quantity** as
the DES internal-MTTC (the mean of three action-event durations —
`SCAN_PORT`/`EXPLOIT_VULN`/`BRUTE_FORCE` — per
[`../specs/metrics_semantics.md`](../specs/metrics_semantics.md) §(a)). The
primer's claim that the Petri net gives "a second route to the same MTTC
numbers" is **false**: it is a *new* metric that shares a name, not a re-
derivation of the simulator's. Fix: define the Petri-CTMC MTTC as its own
named metric, never present it as the DES MTTC.

**A second, sharper wrinkle from Mendonça 2023** (now extracted —
[`../extractions/mendonca2023.md`](../extractions/mendonca2023.md) Concept 1):
the MTD this thesis evaluates is **time-based / periodic** (the SDR scheduler
fires on a fixed interval). A *faithful* encoding of the MTD reset is therefore a
**deterministic** transition (fixed delay), not an exponential one — which makes
the substrate a **deterministic-and-stochastic Petri net (DSPN), not a pure
CTMC**, and the clean `τ = −Q_T⁻¹·1` algebra no longer applies (DSPN needs the
embedded-Markov / supplementary-variable solution that TimeNET / Mercury
implement). This is exactly why Mendonça — modelling periodic MTD — chose DSPN
over SPN/GSPN/SRN. The build faces an explicit fork: **(a)** approximate the MTD
reset as exponential, keeping the clean CTMC and a SNAKES + custom solve, and
*declare the approximation*; or **(b)** adopt a DSPN for fidelity to the periodic
trigger and accept the harder solve (TimeNET/Mercury rather than a hand-rolled
CTMC). Start at (a), treat (b) as the fidelity upgrade — the same analytic-vs-
faithful trade the D4/D2 layering already embodies (§7).

#### 6.4 Comparability & positioning — a second substrate, and a spec change

**Verdict: MAJOR (governance).** [`../specs/architecture.md`](../specs/architecture.md)
§(f)/(i)/(j) currently scopes Petri-net as *parallel-not-primary* and "not a step
inside L1/L2." Building it as a primary L3 analytical baseline **adds a second
substrate column at L4 and extends the comparability boundary**: a CTMC MTTC and
a DES Monte-Carlo MTTC are *within-substrate-incomparable in magnitude*
(§(d)) — only qualitative/ranking comparison survives. There is also a real
tension with the frozen-DES commitment: the RQ is a DES comparison, and the
analytical column does **not** replace it — it is a *complementary* analytical
lens. This requires the architecture decision-blocks to move from "parallel-not-
primary" to "explicit parallel analytical column." **This study flags that
change; it does not make it** (scope discipline — the architecture spec is
Marc-driven). It is go-condition #4.

#### 6.5 Four-class / F1 — do the four nets actually differ, and is the signal real?

**Verdict: MAJOR — the deepest critique.** Because D4 shares one topology and
varies only rate/reward vectors, **the four MTTC/ASR differences are largely
determined by the tactic-share weights put in, not discovered by the CTMC** —
the discrimination is *input*, not *output*. Compounding it: the corpus signal is
already thin (mean technique JSD 0.317 vs null p95 0.148, ~2.1×; operator-dedup
0.315 vs 0.185 at n=29 —
[`../specs/02_gasp_schema.md`](../specs/02_gasp_schema.md) §(g)), the
tactic-quotient erases the F2 long tails the discrimination partly rides on, and
`double_extortion`'s impact rate is grounded on `observation_count = 1` edges
(thin). The honest framing: D4's output is a **sensitivity analysis** ("do the
class orderings hold across the notional rate band, and do they survive the
operator-dedup corpus?"), **not a prediction**. This is the same discipline F5 of
the L2 synthesis already imposed; it carries forward unchanged.

### 7. The recommended design — a layered substrate, not a single net

The four encodings are not competitors to choose between; they are **layers of
one substrate**, and the layering is what answers the critique:

- **D4 (shared-SRN tactic-quotient) is the primary analytical substrate.** Four
  CTMCs over one shared reachability graph → closed-form MTTC / ASR / SRN-reward
  for all four classes, including `infrastructure_setup` (non-objective absorb:
  a competing eviction / C2-established / max-dwell transition, per Bland's
  success-and-blocked-place precedent and Outkin's detection-reset template).
  This is the headline deliverable.
- **D2 (faithful workflow-net), in its single-token reduced projection, is the
  fidelity cross-validator.** Run it — in simulation where it must — on at least
  one class, and **compare its MTTC to D4's analytical MTTC**. The gap *quantifies
  what the tactic-quotient + Markov abstraction costs*. This directly answers the
  "you threw away AND/OR and the technique distinction" critique (§6.1, §6.3):
  the abstraction error is *measured*, not hidden. It is also where the Tesla
  AND-golden and GAP Decision 2 are honoured.
- **D3 (per-technique single-token slice) is a sanity floor** for the two classes
  where a defensible recurring corridor exists (`pure_steal`, `pure_impediment`):
  an independent closed-form MTTC on a mechanically-selected high-recurrence
  spine that should bracket D4's class MTTC. Where it disagrees with D4, that is
  a finding about the tactic-quotient, not a bug.

The scientific posture is the strength here: **D4 gives the analytical answer,
D2 gives the faithful-but-simulated answer, and the distance between them is a
contribution** — "how much does a tactic-quotient CTMC distort the MTTC of a
CTI-derived attacker, relative to a faithful concurrent workflow-net?" is a
question no paper in §3 has asked, because none of them grounded the *attacker*
structure in CTI.

### 8. Feasibility verdict — GO-CONDITIONAL

Analytical Petri-net evaluation of the four GASP classes is **feasible**, via the
layered D4-primary / D2-cross-validator / D3-floor substrate, subject to four
go-conditions. None is a blocker; each caps or frames the claim:

1. **Structural-MTTC under a notional-rate regime, written down.** Rates are
   *purely notional* — the corpus cannot ground them, not even ordinally (88 % of
   edges are `observation_count = 1`; §6.2). The baseline is therefore
   **uniform rates with the MTTC read as a structural metric** (steps-to-
   absorption over the corpus-grounded topology), `observation_count` admitted at
   most as a cosmetic `1 / 2 / ≥3` tie-break, every metric a sensitivity band; a
   *timing* MTTC requires exogenous (Outkin-style) rates, declared and swept.
   Recorded in [`../specs/provenance.md`](../specs/provenance.md) **before** any
   number is quoted. (§6.2)
2. **Discrimination-as-input pre-registered.** D4's output is framed as
   sensitivity analysis (orderings stable across `λ₀` and across the
   operator-dedup corpus), not prediction. (§6.5)
3. **Abstraction error measured, not assumed.** Build D2's reduced projection on
   ≥ 1 class and report the D4-vs-D2 MTTC gap before committing the analytical
   numbers as the L4 column. (§7)
4. **Architecture re-positioned.** `architecture.md` §(f)/(i)/(j) decision-blocks
   move from "parallel-not-primary" to "explicit parallel analytical column,"
   and the second-substrate comparability boundary is stated. Marc-driven.
   (§6.4)

If condition 3 reveals the tactic-quotient distorts MTTC beyond the inter-class
deltas being measured, the verdict degrades to **NO-GO at tactic granularity**,
and the fallback is D3-style per-class hand-curated slices (the primer's
6-node-cone approach) for the two classes that admit them, with the other two
reported as structurally untractable for analytical encoding — itself a
defensible negative result about what a thin CTI corpus can support.
