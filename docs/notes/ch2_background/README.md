# ch2_background — notes feeding the Background chapter

## What this chapter does

The background chapter (ch2 in the ratified structure, between the introduction and
the literature review) carries **the inherited platform** — the moving-target-defence
simulator this work extends, described so a reader understands what it does before
the literature review narrows onto the gap. Its natural sections are the ones the
supervisor named (2026-08-11): the network model, the defence mechanisms, the
inherited baseline attacker, and the prior works in the lineage. This is *existing
things*, presented for comprehension, not methodology — the ruling that moved the
simulator out of the methodology chapter and into a background chapter of its own.
(Whole-document guidance: [`../_writing_guide.md`](../_writing_guide.md).)

What lands here: prose that explains the inherited substrate — the simulator's
network/defence/attacker models and the Brown → Zhang → Ho → Tay lineage — at the
altitude a reader needs to follow the rest of the dissertation. The technical
detail that a reader would need the repo to follow stays in
[`../../implementation/`](../../implementation/) (the substrate primer, the metrics
semantics, the boundary records); a note earns a place here only when the
*explanation* of the inherited platform is itself dissertation prose. Rubric-gated
([`../../workflows/notes_rubric.md`](../../workflows/notes_rubric.md)).

**Placement note (2026-08-14):** the gap-statement and precedent-survey notes that
previously sat here moved to [`../ch3_lit_review/`](../ch3_lit_review/) when the
background and literature-review chapters were separated — those are the literature
review's *occupying move* (naming and narrowing onto the gap), not descriptions of
the inherited platform. Background describes what was inherited; the literature
review argues what the field is missing. Expect most background notes to arrive when
the chapter is drafted; until then the platform's technical record lives in
`implementation/`.
