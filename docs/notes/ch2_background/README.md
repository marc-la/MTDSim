## What this chapter does

The background chapter is a **story that narrows onto the gap**. Each category of prior work is told chronologically — a method, what it achieved, the limitation that forced the next method — never a neutral catalogue: every cited work earns a sentence of evaluation (what is good about it, why the field moved past it). The categories converge so that, by the chapter's end, the reader concludes the gap themselves before being told: none of the surveyed work evaluates MTD against a post-ingress, objective-driven campaign, and there is a demonstrated need for exactly what this thesis does. Citation selection per category: the *first* method (sets the direction), the *most established* (test of time), and the *latest* (current-year where possible). The introduction will later compress this chapter's narrowing into a landmark-to-landmark sprint — so notes here should identify which works are the landmarks.
