# ch5_experimental_setup — notes feeding the Experimental setup chapter

## What this chapter does

The experimental-setup chapter (ch5 since the 2026-09-04 restructure, Marc's
ruling; it was the methodology chapter's §4.3 and keeps that section's structure
unchanged) says **what is done with the attacker model** before any result is
read: what the evaluation must show (the two-part burden of proof — ranking
stability and ranking divergence — and the three-grade instrument every reported
comparison is scored on, committed before scoring); the metrics, with the
inherited suite's comparability boundary and the APT-specific supplementary
measures; and the dimensions and the two experiment families (the prior-model
comparison and the fresh evaluation). It is the *evaluate* sub-question's
methodological home: the attacker-model chapter answers capture and model, this
chapter answers evaluate, and the results chapter reports the numbers against
the criteria pre-committed here. Its further shape is Marc's to settle.
(Whole-document guidance: [`../_writing_guide.md`](../_writing_guide.md).)

What lands here: *experimental-design arguments* — what the evaluation must
demonstrate, how comparisons are graded, and the discipline that gates a
metric's reporting. Modelling and realisation arguments stay in
[`../ch4_methods/`](../ch4_methods/); results framing in
[`../ch6_results/`](../ch6_results/); interpretation in
[`../ch7_discussion/`](../ch7_discussion/). Rubric-gated
([`../../workflows/notes_rubric.md`](../../workflows/notes_rubric.md)).
