# ch6_results — notes feeding the Results chapter

## What this chapter does

The results chapter (ch6 since the 2026-09-04 restructure; was ch5) reports the
empirical outcome in two ratified movements (2026-08-11 structure): the **sensitivity analysis** — the declared-parameter
regime made visible, a results preamble tabling the arbitrarily-set parameters,
their swept ranges and observed effects before any headline is read — and the
**MTD evaluation** itself: how the defence conditions perform against the
movement attacker versus the inherited baseline, scored in the pre-committed
grading vocabulary and against the pre-declared burden of proof (both staged in
[`../ch5_experimental_setup/`](../ch5_experimental_setup/)). Comparability discipline applies:
within-substrate comparison only. Outcomes are reported against the declared
criteria rather than sympathetically — a ranking that fails to move, or a
sensitivity that kills a conclusion, is reported as the finding it is.
(Whole-document guidance: [`../_writing_guide.md`](../_writing_guide.md).)

What lands here: *results framing* — how a found result is stated, bounded and
defended. Experimental-design arguments belong in `ch5_experimental_setup`;
interpretation against the field belongs in `ch7_discussion`. Rubric-gated
([`../../workflows/notes_rubric.md`](../../workflows/notes_rubric.md)).

Current notes: [`defence_ranking_inversion.md`](defence_ranking_inversion.md) —
the headline result: the defence ranking inverts between the inherited and the
profiled attacker, so an evaluation's recommendation is a function of its threat
model. Expect most content to arrive as the experiments run.
