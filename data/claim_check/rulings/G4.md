# G4: a citation whose source argues against the sentence

**Applied 2026-10-17 (Marc, accept all):** items 1, 2a, 3, 4 as recommended; 2b kept; neither option taken.

**Rule.** Where the source supports part of a sentence and argues against another part, the citation goes on the part it supports. The rest stands on its own basis: the formalism, our repair, or a stated choice. New words are added only where an examiner who knows the source would otherwise catch the conflict. Items 1, 3 and 4 are in Chapters 2 and 4, and item 2 is in Chapter 5, so all are proposals. The evidence was re-read in the sources and the release code on 2026-10-17.
