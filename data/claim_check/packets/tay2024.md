### U0787 (prose, L8992) tier H
*Discussion > Implications for MTD evaluation*

> Tay et al. tested MTDShield in its training environment, against the baseline attacker, and compared it with no MTD [tay2024].

Context before: [U0785] At 200 s, MTDShield chooses service diversity in most of its decisions against either attacker (Section <subsec:eff-cross-arm>), and service diversity is the only MTD mechanism the baseline attacker loses time to (Section <sec:disc-mtd-performance>). [U0786] Its choices are consistent with a policy that learned to deploy the MTD mechanism that disrupts its training attacker most (<thr:mtdshield>).

Context after: [U0788] Like a test on training data, a test in the training environment cannot separate a deployment strategy that generalises to other attackers from one fitted to its training attacker. [U0789] An MTD evaluation should therefore test a learned deployment strategy against an attacker model other than the one it was trained against, beside the random deployment strategy over the same MTD mechanisms, which shows what the learning adds.
